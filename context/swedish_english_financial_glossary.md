# Swedish → English Financial & Accounting Glossary

A working glossary for reading Swedish corporate financial statements, focused on what you encounter in a Swedish AB (aktiebolag, limited company). Compiled from a Swedish AB's annual report and quarterly reports, supplemented with broader Swedish accounting / Skatteverket terminology.

**Conventions used below**
- *Swedish term* — **English term** — short note, when useful
- "Lit." = literal translation; the listed English term is the one used in practice
- BAS account ranges refer to the [BAS chart of accounts](https://www.bas.se/english/chart-of-account/) used by virtually every Swedish AB
- Swedish numbers use space as thousands separator and comma as decimal (1 234 567,89 = 1,234,567.89)
- Negative numbers in raw GL reports are often shown with a leading minus (−); on the equity / liability side of a Balansrapport, credit balances print as negative by convention even though the underlying amount is positive

## 12. Number, date & currency conventions

- **Thousands / decimal**: `1 234 567,89` ⇒ `1,234,567.89`
- **Negative numbers**: `−47 154,00` (with a true minus sign, often `–` or `−`, not a hyphen)
- **Currency**: SEK / kr — there is no separate subunit symbol; öre is the 1/100 subunit
- **Dates**: ISO format `YYYY-MM-DD` is standard, e.g. `2025-12-31`
- **Financial year**: usually the calendar year; a Swedish AB can choose a "brutet" (broken / non-calendar) räkenskapsår, but it must be 12 months
- **Quarter**: kvartal — Q1 = 1 Jan–31 Mar, etc.
